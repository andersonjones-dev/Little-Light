# Little Light: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

Little Light is made by Anderson Jones. It is a Christian faith and screen-time companion for families with children aged 4–10.

## In short

Little Light keeps everything on your phone. It does not collect, send or sell anyone's information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It does not ask for internet access.

## What is stored on the phone

- Children's first names or nicknames and a chosen colour
- Activity progress: stories, verses, kindness challenges and the garden
- The parent's settings and a 4-digit parent PIN
- The list of fun apps the parent chose, and today's fun-time minutes

This information stays in Little Light's private storage on the device. Android's own device backup may include it if you have turned that on. Deleting the app deletes this information.

## Usage access and Display over other apps

A parent can turn on the faith-first helper. With the parent's permission, it uses:

- **Usage access**, only to know which app is currently open, so it can tell when a fun app the parent chose is opened and count fun-time minutes.
- **Display over other apps**, only to show Little Light's "Faith time first" or "Fun time is done" screen over those apps.

Little Light does not read what is on the screen, see messages or keystrokes, or record anything. This information is used only on the phone and is never stored as a history or sent anywhere. The parent can turn these permissions off at any time in Android Settings.

## Children

Little Light is designed for children to use with their parents. It does not collect personal information from children. It has no chat, social features, public profiles or ads, and no links that take children outside the app.
